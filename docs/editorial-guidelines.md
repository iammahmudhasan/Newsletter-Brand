# AI Signal — Editorial Guidelines & Issue Structure

## Core Promise
Every issue of AI Signal must be readable in **5 minutes** and give engineers and founders actionable clarity on what happened, why it matters, and what to build.

---

## The 4-Part Structure

### 1. Three Critical AI Developments
* **Format:** Bullet points with clear headings (A, B, C).
* **Requirements:**
  * Must include: What Happened, Why It Matters, and Source Link.
  * No press release regurgitation — provide original technical interpretation.
  * Focus on foundation models, training economics, serving latency, and compute infra.

### 2. One Research Paper Explained
* **Format:**
  * Title and authors with institutional affiliation.
  * **Core Hypothesis:** The fundamental research question.
  * **Methodology:** How the experiment was designed.
  * **Results:** Exact metric deltas on recognized benchmarks (e.g. GSM8K, MATH, HumanEval).
  * **Caveats & Limitations:** Where the method fails or incurs latency/memory penalties.
  * **Why It Matters to Builders:** The engineering takeaway.

### 3. One Startup & Product Signal
* **Format:**
  * **The Signal:** Emerging commercial pattern or tool adoption spike.
  * **The Opportunity:** Market vacuum or infrastructure need.
  * **Founder Takeaway:** Strategic guidance for technical entrepreneurs.

### 4. One Actionable Takeaway
* **Format:**
  * A concrete script, prompt architecture, or benchmarking formula readers can test in their own codebase over the weekend.
